### Category 1: Efficiency, Waste, & OEE Component Analysis

**1. What are the central tendency and dispersion metrics (Mean, Median, Standard Deviation) for the efficiency_ratio?**
* **Data:** `efficiency_ratio` column from `Manufacturing_Final.csv`.
* **Action:** Calculated descriptive statistics.
* **Insight:** The process has a Mean Efficiency of **84.0%** and a Standard Deviation of **15.2%**. This high deviation indicates significant process instability—predictability is a major issue.

**2. What is the total quantified "Unexplained Time Loss" (Hidden Waste) in minutes?**
* **Data:** `duration_minutes`, `total_downtime_min`, `min_batch_time`.
* **Action:** Calculated `sum(duration_minutes - total_downtime_min - min_batch_time)`.
* **Insight:** The total hidden waste is **2,663 minutes** (over 44 hours). This represents "ghost time" not captured by downtime logs, indicating slow standard work or unrecorded micro-stops.

**3. What is the true "Performance" rate of the line (as an OEE component)?**
* **Data:** `min_batch_time`, `net_production_min`.
* **Action:** Calculated `Total_Ideal_Time / Total_Net_Production_Time`.
* **Insight:** The Performance rate is **81.6%**. Even when the line is running, it runs at only ~82% of its design speed.

**4. What is the true "Availability" rate of the line (as an OEE component)?**
* **Data:** `duration_minutes`, `net_production_min`.
* **Action:** Calculated `Total_Net_Production_Time / Total_Planned_Time`.
* **Insight:** The Availability rate is **72.9%**, meaning the line is only producing product for roughly three-quarters of its scheduled time.

**5. What is the "Perfect Batch" count, and what is its associated financial opportunity cost?**

* **Action:** Counted rows meeting criteria.
